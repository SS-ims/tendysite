About Tendy Woodland

Your Trusted Partner in Quality, Food Safety & Business Excellence
At Tendy Woodland Services, we help businesses achieve excellence through quality management, food safety compliance, and internationally recognized certification systems.
Established in 2023, we are a Zimbabwean consultancy firm dedicated to supporting organizations in building robust management systems, improving operational performance, and unlocking sustainable growth opportunities. Through strategic partnerships with SHEMS Consultancy, LDC Global (South Africa), and Certisure Solutions, we deliver professional consultancy, training, auditing, and certification support services across Zimbabwe and South Africa.
Whether your goal is to strengthen customer confidence, enter new markets, improve compliance, or achieve certification, our team provides practical, affordable, and customized solutions designed around your business needs.

Meet Our Principal Consultant

Ennety Chemhere

Ennety Chemhere is a Quality and Food Safety Specialist with more than 18 years of experience in food manufacturing, the beef industry, beverages, catering, consultancy, and auditing. She has successfully led quality improvement, food safety compliance, certification, auditing, and training initiatives across Zimbabwe, Malawi, Afghanistan, and South Africa.

As Principal Consultant at Tendy Woodland Services, Ennety supports organizations in implementing internationally recognized systems including ISO 9001:2015, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, and HACCP. Her expertise in auditing, risk management, continuous improvement, and food safety culture development helps businesses achieve certification, operational excellence, and sustainable growth.
